# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Z.E.D. is a project for people who want to build a thoughtful assistant. We are
committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good faith, ask clarifying questions, and critique the work rather than
  the person.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy: never publish API keys, phone numbers, contacts, private
  audio, device logs, or creator codes.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and move disagreements to a focused,
  technical discussion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Doxxing or publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, spam, or bad-faith argument.
- Sharing credentials, private audio, or personal data without consent.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
and any project space where someone is representing Z.E.D. It also applies when
project participants interact in public spaces about the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact listed on the
repository owner profile. Include what happened, where, and who was involved.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Do not open a public
issue for a conduct report that contains personal information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. a private warning or clarification;
2. removing or editing comments, commits, or contributions;
3. a temporary restriction on participation; or
4. a permanent ban from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions are made in good faith and focus on the safety of the
community. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
